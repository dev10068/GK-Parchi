   * **English:** Data from the Election Commission’s ERONET software audit of Delhi's draft electoral rolls revealed that Muslims, who form 13.6% of electors in Delhi, accounted for 20.3% of those flagged for 'logical discrepancies' and 30% of those flagged under five specific criteria indicative of family size. In contrast, Bihar (which did not implement logical discrepancy checks) saw Muslims account for 18.4% of deleted electors, aligning closely with their 17.7% share of the population. This highlights systemic biases or technical anomalies in algorithmic voter roll scrubbing, making it a critical topic for governance and electoral studies in competitive exams.
