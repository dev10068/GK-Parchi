According to the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD), the landfall process is complete, and the weather system has moved entirely over land. Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. The government intensified disaster preparedness by shifting 4,711 pregnant women to health facilities and deploying 15 National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) teams. The Roads and Buildings (R&B) department kept emergency teams ready to ensure road safety and swift restoration of traffic. Additionally, schools in 16 coastal districts were closed, and 609 schools were converted into relief shelters. 17,054 NCC, NSS, and Scouts volunteers were mobilized for relief and rescue operations.
