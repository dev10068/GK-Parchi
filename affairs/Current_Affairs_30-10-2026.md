**[Mysuru, Postal Service, Certificates]**
The Mysuru City Corporation (MCC) has signed a significant agreement with India Post to deliver birth and death certificates to applicants' doorsteps via postal service for a fee of ₹100. This initiative marks a step towards making government services more accessible to citizens, especially those residing in remote areas. It is an effort towards Digital India and good governance, freeing people from the need to visit government offices, thereby saving both time and money. There are plans to extend this service across India, establishing a uniform and efficient service delivery model. This aligns with the principles of the central government's 'Doorstep Delivery' or 'Citizen-Centric Service' model, aiming to simplify government processes and improve efficiency. For competitive exams, this can be important for questions on urban local bodies, e-governance initiatives, and the role of India Post.

**[Cyclone, Odisha, Relief Efforts]**
The very severe cyclonic storm 'Arnab' has caused widespread devastation in Odisha, leading to the evacuation of over 55,000 people across nine districts to safer locations. The cyclone has resulted in extensive damage to homes, agricultural land, and infrastructure. The state government has deployed National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) teams for relief and rescue operations. The Chief Minister reviewed the situation and issued directives for prompt assistance to affected areas. This incident highlights the increasing threat of natural disasters in India and the need for effective disaster management strategies. For exams, this is relevant for questions on disaster management, the role of NDRF/SDRF, cyclone naming conventions, and the vulnerability of India's coastal states.

**[KG Basin, Gas Dispute, Supreme Court, Mediator]**
In the KG Basin gas migration dispute, the Supreme Court has appointed Justice Ranjana Desai as the sole mediator. The dispute is between Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) and the central government, with the Centre alleging that RIL "siphoned" gas that had migrated from neighboring blocks of state-owned ONGC into RIL's KG-D6 block. RIL has refuted these claims. The Bench also requested the mediator to take assistance from two domain experts. This judicial intervention plays a crucial role in resolving complex natural resource disputes between large corporate and government entities. For exams, this is important for questions related to the role of the Supreme Court, the mediation process, natural resource management, and the regulatory framework of the energy sector in India.
