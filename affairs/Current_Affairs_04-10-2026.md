*   **English:** The Tamil Nadu government has launched the 'Vettri Payanam' scheme to assist women passengers travelling to their workplaces. However, the scheme has faced legal challenges before the Madras High Court, where a private bus operator from Erode has urged the court to declare it unconstitutional. The petitioner claims that providing no-fare travel exclusively in government buses causes a severe dent in the business of private operators, potentially forcing them to shut down operations.
