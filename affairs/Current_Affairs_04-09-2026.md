   * **English Translation:** Amidst severe disruptions caused by the Iran-U.S. war, Syria is reportedly pitching itself as an overland corridor for energy and goods transport, offering a viable alternative to the highly volatile Strait of Hormuz. U.S. leadership lauded this development as "GREAT," as it bypasses the traditional maritime chokepoint through which a significant portion of global oil passes. The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most critical oil transit bottleneck, and Syria's emergence as an alternative logistics route marks a major shift in Middle Eastern geopolitics and global supply chains. For competitive exams, this is a key geography and international relations update regarding critical trade routes and energy security.
