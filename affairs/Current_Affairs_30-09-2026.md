### PIB & Government Schemes (पीआईबी और सरकारी योजनाएं)

1. **[CAFE Norms, Fuel Efficiency, Passenger Vehicles]** 
   - **What happened / क्या हुआ:** The Central Government has officially notified the new Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE) norms, significantly tightening the fuel-efficiency targets for passenger vehicles manufactured and sold in India. Under this updated framework, automobile manufacturers are mandated to progressively lower carbon dioxide emissions and improve the average mileage of their entire fleet. Additionally, the new policy framework introduces attractive regulatory incentives for cleaner alternative fuels and advanced green technologies, explicitly including flex-fuel and ethanol-powered vehicles to encourage sustainable automotive manufacturing.
   - **Background & Context / पृष्ठभूमि:** These stringent norms are part of India’s broader climate commitments under international treaties and India’s Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) submitted to the UNFCCC to reduce carbon emission intensity. The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), in active consultation with the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) and automotive industry stakeholders, formulated these guidelines to curb urban air pollution and decrease the nation's heavy economic reliance on crude oil imports.
   - **Key Numbers & Stats / मुख्य आँकड़े:** The revised CAFE regulations mandate lower average corporate CO2 emissions per kilometer, requiring automakers to align with strict targets that scale progressively over the next block of years, with specific compliance checkpoints established for alternative energy vehicles.
   - **Organizations & Places / संगठन और स्थान:** Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), and domestic passenger vehicle manufacturers across India.
   - **Exam Relevance / परीक्षा महत्व:** Essential for UPSC, SSC CGL, and Banking exams under government policies, environmental regulations, sustainable development, and current economic affairs.

2. **[Secunderabad Cantonment, Freehold Titles, Defense Land]** 
   - **What happened / क्या हुआ:** Residents of the Secunderabad Cantonment area, who have been living on defense land for multiple generations, have intensified their demands seeking permanent freehold titles for their properties. This persistent public demand, strongly championed by the Cantonment Vikas Manch, forms an integral part of a comprehensive four-point proposal aimed at restructuring, modernizing, and reforming the administrative and civic management of civilian pockets located inside military cantonments across the country.
   - **Background & Context / पृष्ठभूमि:** Historically, civilian residents within military cantonments across India have faced unique land tenure challenges, as the land legally belongs to the Ministry of Defense (MoD). Consequently, residents face severe restrictions on property modifications, commercial usage, and obtaining bank loans against property mortgages. The central government has been evaluating proposals to excise civilian areas from certain cantonments and merge them with local municipal bodies to streamline civil administration.
   - **Key Numbers & Stats / मुख्य आँकड़े:** The comprehensive restructuring proposal includes a four-point charter focusing on freehold land rights, upgrading municipal infrastructure, integrating civic services with local urban bodies, and ensuring equitable budgetary allocations for residents.
   - **Organizations & Places / संगठन और स्थान:** Secunderabad Cantonment, Cantonment Vikas Manch, Ministry of Defense (MoD), and local civic bodies in Telangana.
   - **Exam Relevance / परीक्षा महत्व:** Important for SSC CGL, State PSCs, and Polity sections concerning urban local governance, defense land management, and citizens' rights.
