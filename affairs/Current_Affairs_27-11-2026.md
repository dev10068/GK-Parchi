2.  **[NGT, अपशिष्ट प्रबंधन, केरल]**

    *   **English Translation:** **[NGT, Waste Management, Kerala]**
        A report by an NGT-appointed amicus curiae has highlighted significant gaps in solid and liquid waste management in Kerala. The report stated that about four-fifths of the sanitary waste generated is not scientifically treated. Out of the total 203.66 tonnes of sanitary waste generated daily, only 42.1 tonnes per day are treated. The report also pointed out that even after all projects are completed, the treatment capacity will only reach 113.25 tonnes per day, which is significantly less than the amount of waste generated. This report is crucial for exams in the context of environmental protection, the implementation of waste management policies, and the role of the NGT. It also reflects challenges under government programs like Swachh Bharat Abhiyan.
