**[Supreme Court, Railways, Compensation]**
The Supreme Court has ruled that the non-recovery of a train ticket from a deceased passenger cannot defeat a compensation claim. The Bench noted that after the parents of the deceased filed the claim application before the tribunal, the authorities prepared the investigation report. This significant ruling means that even in the absence of a physical ticket, dependents of passengers who lose their lives in railway accidents or incidents may still be eligible for compensation, provided other evidence of travel exists. This decision strengthens consumer rights and broadens the scope of compensation provided by railways to passengers. For competitive exams, this is crucial for questions on important Supreme Court judgments, consumer protection laws, and railway regulations.
