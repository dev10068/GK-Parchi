Tele-MANAS (Tele Mental Health Assistance and Networking Across States) is an initiative by the Government of India, launched under the National Mental Health Program (NMHP). Its objective is to provide 24/7 free tele-mental health services across the country, particularly enhancing access to mental health support in rural and remote areas. The establishment of the centre in Tirupati is part of the government's broader goal to strengthen mental healthcare services under the National Health Mission (NHM). For exams, the Tele-MANAS scheme is a crucial government initiative addressing mental healthcare provision in India.
