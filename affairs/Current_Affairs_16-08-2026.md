**English:**

Legally, the BCI is a statutory body constituted under the Advocates Act, 1961, regulating legal education and professional conduct in India. The episode highlights key constitutional facets regarding the Fundamental Right to Freedom of Speech and Expression under Article 19(1)(a) versus professional regulatory boundaries.

**English:**

In Indian constitutional law, showing respect to the National Anthem and National Flag is a Fundamental Duty enshrined under Article 51A(a). Furthermore, Section 3 of the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971 penalizes intentionally preventing the singing of the National Anthem or causing disturbances. Historically, the Constituent Assembly adopted 'Jana Gana Mana' as the National Anthem and 'Vande Mataram' (authored by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay) as the National Song on January 24, 1950.

**English:**

India's 'Big Four' venomous snakes responsible for majority of snakebite mortalities are the Spectacled Cobra, Common Krait, Russell's Viper, and Saw-scaled Viper. The World Health Organization (WHO) classifies snakebite envenoming as a high-priority Neglected Tropical Disease (NTD), targeting a 50% reduction in global mortality and morbidity by 2030 through rapid access to polyvalent anti-snake venom (ASV).

#### **[Nihang Sikhs, Sikh History, Martial Tradition]**
**हिंदी:**

**English:**

Historically regarded as the 'Guru's Army', the Nihang order dates back to the creation of the Khalsa by the tenth Sikh Guru, Guru Gobind Singh, in 1699. Distinct for their electric-blue attire, towering turbans (*dumalla*) adorned with steel *chakkars*, and traditional weaponry, the Nihangs historically functioned as the vanguard of Sikh defensive forces during foreign invasions. They are also the principal custodians of the indigenous Indian martial art form known as *Gatka*.

#### **[Salmonella Diagnostics, CIDT, Antimicrobial Resistance]**
**हिंदी:**

**English:**  
The transition toward Culture-Independent Diagnostic Tests (CIDTs) for detecting *Salmonella* bacterial infections has accelerated clinical diagnosis but introduced major hurdles for public health epidemiological tracking and whole-genome sequencing. *Salmonella* is a genus of gram-negative, rod-shaped bacteria responsible for salmonellosis and typhoid fever worldwide. While rapid molecular tests (like PCR) quickly detect pathogen DNA, the lack of live bacterial isolates hinders laboratory surveillance of Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) profiles and multi-state outbreak traceback. According to the WHO, non-typhoidal *Salmonella* remains one of the four key global causes of diarrhoeal diseases.
