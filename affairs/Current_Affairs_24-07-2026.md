**[Waqf Properties, UMEED Portal, Digitization]**
Over 6.17 Lakh Waqf Properties Approved on UMEED Portal
Under a significant initiative by the Government of India, over 6.17 lakh Waqf properties have been successfully approved on the "UMEED" portal. This portal is part of the government's ongoing efforts to digitize and streamline Waqf property records, aiming to bring transparency and efficiency to the management of these assets. The UMEED portal, a component of the Waqf Property Management System (WPMS), enables various Waqf boards and stakeholders to upload data, monitor property status, and access relevant information. This initiative is crucial in empowering Waqf Boards established under the Waqf Act, 1995, and ensuring the effective utilization of these properties for the benefit of the Muslim community. From a competitive exam perspective, this is important for questions related to e-governance, minority welfare, digitization of land records, and specific government portals like UMEED.
