    *   **Blocking of Battery Management Apps**
        The Centre has ordered the blocking of battery management apps used for e-rickshaws, following incidents where these apps were used to remotely disable e-rickshaws, leading to extortion.

    *   **New Notice to Telegram**
        The Information & Broadcasting Ministry has issued a fresh notice to Telegram regarding pirated content. The Ministry has demanded an Action Taken Report within 15 days, stating that Telegram must abide by IT Act and IT Rules.
