[Agniveer, Agnipath, MoD]

The scheme aims to lower the average age of the armed forces, making them younger and fitter, while also providing trained and disciplined youth back to society. It is also designed to rationalize defence expenditure and contribute to nation-building. For competitive exams, candidates should be aware of the key features of the Agnipath scheme (recruitment process, service tenure, financial package, post-'retirement' benefits) and the objectives behind its implementation.
