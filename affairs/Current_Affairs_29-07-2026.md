    *   **[Neurosurgery, Deep Brain Stimulation, Kailash Bhatia]**
        In the EAN 2026 interview, Kailash Bhatia stated that in his view, the biggest advance that has changed lives is functional neurosurgery, citing examples like deep brain stimulation (DBS) surgery. DBS is a neurosurgical procedure involving the implantation of electrodes within certain areas of the brain that generate electrical impulses. These impulses help regulate abnormal brain activity and are used to treat movement disorders such as Parkinson's disease, essential tremor, and dystonia. This highlights advanced medical technology and breakthroughs in the field of neuroscience. This is crucial for exam questions related to science and technology, medical innovation, and their impact on human health.
